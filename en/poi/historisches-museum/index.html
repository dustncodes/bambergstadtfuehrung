The Historical Museum Bamberg presents key chapters of the city’s past within the Domberg ensemble, right beside the Cathedral and the New Residence. The official pages are your current source for program updates, services and visit planning. [1][3]

Profile & location

Housed in the Alte Hofhaltung on Domplatz, the museum unites historical collections with historic architecture. Arcades and the courtyard connect quickly to other Domberg highlights. For precise access information, see the museum and city portals. [1][3]

As a museum of city and regional history, it spans themes from early settlement to modern times. The linked portals outline this breadth across cultural history and the interplay of city, church, rule and everyday life. [1][2][5]

What to expect

  • Context for the city: Objects and images set Bamberg within Franconian and European narratives; the breadth of topics is sketched on the official pages. [1][5]
  • Art & culture links: Displays pick up artistic, religious and civic traditions—from representative pieces to everyday history. [2][4][5]
  • Domberg context: The Domplatz location makes combo‑visits easy; short walks and clear sightlines tie museum themes to the urban fabric. [1][3]

Plan your visit

  • Hours & tickets: See the official website for times, prices and seasonal adjustments. museum.bamberg.de [1]
  • Current program: Special exhibitions, events and learning offers are listed on the portals. [1][3][5]
  • Services: Practical info for groups, families and schools is summarized on the museum entries. [1][4][5]
